The history of educational technology at Minnesota

Minnesota has an outsized place in the history of educational computing: the state that gave the world The Oregon Trail is also home to the university whose campus mascot lent its name to the Gopher protocol — for a moment in the early 1990s, the front door to the internet. This timeline traces how teaching technology arrived, changed, and was contested at the University of Minnesota. Every event cites its sources.
